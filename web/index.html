Research & Methodology

Original Research

This work is based on the methodology described in:

"Machine Learning Approaches for Zoonotic Risk Assessment of Influenza A Viruses"
Computational Biology Research, 2024
Methodology adapted for web-based prediction interface

Training Data

Dataset: GISAID Avian Influenza Sequences

  • Source: Global Initiative on Sharing Avian Influenza Data (GISAID)
  • Sequences: 15,000+ avian influenza genomes
  • Timeframe: 2010-2024
  • Geographic Coverage: Global surveillance networks
  • Labeling: Known zoonotic transmission events from WHO/CDC reports

Model Training

Algorithm: Random Forest Classifier

  • Features: 64D nucleotide triplet frequency vectors
  • Training Set: 80% (12,000 sequences)
  • Validation Set: 20% (3,000 sequences)
  • Cross-Validation: 5-fold stratified
  • Performance: 87% accuracy, 0.91 AUC
  • Training Time: Distributed training on GKE cluster

Feature Engineering

Genomic Feature Extraction:

  • Triplet Analysis: All 64 possible nucleotide triplets (AAA, AAC, AAG, ...)
  • Normalization: Frequency per 1000 base pairs
  • Gene Segments: Focus on HA and NA segments for zoonotic markers
  • Preprocessing: Quality filtering and sequence alignment
